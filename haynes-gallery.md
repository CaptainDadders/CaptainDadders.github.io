### 1804 — Outbound

**Shooting For the Red Leggings** — *January 16, 1804*  
Camp River Dubois (Wood River). Shooting match between Corps men and locals; Reuben Field wins a pair of red leggings for the fort.  
Image: `https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5a5f97febce17635d27bafb0/1517416889099-OVYI0E1ID9G9XOPY6RH5/Shooting.png`

**Ordway's Mast** — *June 4, 1804*  
Near present Jefferson City, MO. Keelboat mast snaps in a sycamore limb; Sgt. Ordway later admitted he was steering alone when it happened.  
Image: `https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5a5f97febce17635d27bafb0/1517416741277-ICL8QH72BJPGZMI7MX07/OrdwaysMast.png`

**Near Miss** — *1804, undated* (Missouri River, outbound)  
The keelboat grounds on a hidden sandbar and tips dangerously to larboard; men scramble to keep her upright before the sand gives way.  
Image: `https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5a5f97febce17635d27bafb0/1517420543558-CLEY0TO8Q8YSOO756RZB/Near-Miss.png`

**York** — *August 24, 1804*  
York as a hunter, carrying a small deer on his back (per Clark's journal entry). First known depiction; underscores his active role in the expedition.  
Image: `https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5a5f97febce17635d27bafb0/1517416231444-OUU4HATZNQ3CMQNI4OTE/York.png`

**Moreau River** — *October 7, 1804*  
Near present Mobridge, SD. Morning hunt: Drouillard reading sign, the Field brothers with horses, men branding trees beside massive sycamores. First grizzly tracks were sighted this day.  
Image: `https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5a5f97febce17635d27bafb0/1517349016474-JKS6FLKAUU2VR36WF39J/Moreau.png`

**Northern Lights — Ft. Mandan** — *November 6, 1804*  
Lewis and Clark, awakened by the guard, viewing the aurora borealis; Fort Mandan still under construction around them.  
Image: `https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5a5f97febce17635d27bafb0/1517416519891-W0G17SH4MI09FYZ99THJ/Northern-Light.png`

**Sacagawea's First Gift** — *November 11, 1804*  
Fort Mandan. Charbonneau arrives with his two Shoshone wives bearing buffalo robes — likely the captains' first encounter with Sacagawea.  
Image: `https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5a5f97febce17635d27bafb0/1517420417454-E66EH846Q06PV15YWAXE/Sac%27sGift-.png`

**Red Shirt** — *1804, undated* (upper Missouri, outbound)  
Sgt. Ordway hunting bison in a red shirt; confronted by a bull, he fires and grazes it, then rain fouls his gun and he returns to camp.  
Image: `https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5a5f97febce17635d27bafb0/1517420645923-9XO487ZVBNFW1SQI1MKG/Red-Shirt.png`

### 1805 — Fort Mandan & Westbound

**Dawn Among the Cottonwoods** — *February 28, 1805*  
Men building six pirogues outside Fort Mandan in pre-dawn frost, preparing for the spring departure. Watch coats, cottonwood logs, ice on the river.  
Image: `https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5a5f97febce17635d27bafb0/1517349361734-2F9UQVMORLS2VLE9UAO3/Dawn.png`

**Sacagawea** — *Spring 1805*  
Portrait of Sacagawea with baby Pomp, in an early plains-style two-deerskin dress with gold-painted yoke, carrying a Hidatsa wood-and-antler rake.  
Image: `https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5a5f97febce17635d27bafb0/1517415805293-G78W3XKBGKKNDYMK9JL9/Sacagawea.png`

### 1805 — Pacific Coast / Return

**Trade At Dismal Niche** — *November 11, 1805*  
Corps pinned by storms in a shallow cove just short of the Pacific. Five Chinook paddle in through tremendous surf and trade 13 salmon for fishhooks; first fresh food in days.  
Image: `https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5a5f97febce17635d27bafb0/1517349613365-P8MLH95FCNFC5EKWMD50/Dismal.png`
